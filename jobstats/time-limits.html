Time Limits By Partition

Each job on the cluster has a time limit associated with it, deciding the maximum amount of time it is allowed to run. Below you will find the time limit set if not specified (the default time limit) and the maximum time limit you can specify for your requested partition.

Partition Default Time Limit Maximum Time Limit
batch-impulse 1-00:00:00 7-00:00:00
batch-warp 1-00:00:00 7-00:00:00
any-interactive 02:00:00 04:00:00
interactive 02:00:00 04:00:00
gpu-interactive 02:00:00 04:00:00
bigmem 1-00:00:00 7-00:00:00
hugemem 1-00:00:00 7-00:00:00

The formats above read as days-hours:minutes:seconds.

My Job Exceeded the Time Limit

If your job reports a TIMEOUT state, your job has been prematurely stopped because your allocated time ran out. By default, interactive jobs allocate 2 hours, and batch jobs allocate 24 hours.

To specify a new time limit, use the --time SLURM directive:

--time=00:02:00   # For 2 hours
--time=00:04:00   # For 4 hours
--time=00:24:00   # For 24 hours
--time=1-00:00:00 # For 1 day

Note that there are maximum time limits imposed depending on your partition. See the section above.
